# Thurayilkunnu Sree Subramanya Swami Temple - Mobile App (Expo / React Native)

A native mobile application for devotees of **Thurayilkunnu Sree Subramanya Swami Temple**.

---

## 📱 How to Run on Your Mobile Phone (Zero Setup Required)

You do **not** need Android Studio, Xcode, Java, or Gradle on your computer!

### Step 1: Install Expo Go on your phone
- **Android**: Install [Expo Go from Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=host.exp.exponent)
- **iOS**: Install [Expo Go from Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/expo-go/id982107779)

### Step 2: Start the Expo Development Server
Open your terminal in this folder and run:
`ash
npx expo start
`

### Step 3: Scan & Experience Live
- **Android**: Open the **Expo Go** app on your phone and tap **Scan QR Code** to scan the QR code printed in your terminal.
- **iOS**: Open the native **Camera app**, point it at the QR code, and tap the prompt to open in **Expo Go**.

The app will load directly onto your phone in seconds with instant hot-reload!

---

## 🪔 Mobile App Features
- **Sanctum Darshan Live Status & Pooja Timings**: Morning (05:00 AM – 10:30 AM) & Evening (05:30 PM – 08:00 PM).
- **Vazhipadu & Pooja Booking**: Devotee name input, full 27 Malayalam Birth Stars (Nakshatras) picker, and instant WhatsApp booking generator.
- **Direct Dialing & GPS Navigation**: One-tap phone call to the temple office and Google Maps route navigation.
- **Sacred Theme Design**: Authentic Nilavilakku Brass & Teakwood styling matching the web portal.
